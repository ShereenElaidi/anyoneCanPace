# Duck outfit art: brief for ChatGPT

**Goal:** 20 clothing pieces for the AnyoneCanPace running-duck mascot. Each one is a separate transparent image that the app stacks on top of the duck to dress it for the weather.

**Attach these to your first message:**

1. `01-duck-reference.png`: the mascot (1024 × 1024, transparent background)
2. `02-placement-map.png`: where each garment sits on the duck
3. `03-current-drafts-to-replace.png`: the current rough versions, to show what each piece is (not the style to copy)

---

## Message 1: the setup (paste this first, with the 3 images)

> I'm attaching my mascot, a running duck (image 1), a placement map (image 2) showing where clothing goes on him, and rough drafts (image 3) of the outfits I need. The drafts only show *what* each piece is. Please don't copy their style.
>
> I need clothing pieces that fit this exact duck in this exact running pose, as **separate transparent layers** I can stack on top of the original duck in an app. I'll ask for one item per message.
>
> Rules for every image:
> - **Square 1024 × 1024 PNG with a fully transparent background.**
> - **Draw only the clothing item.** No duck, no body parts, no shadow, no background, no text.
> - **Same size and position as on image 1**, as if the duck were underneath. If I put your image on top of image 1, the item should sit exactly on the duck: on his head, torso, arms, hands, hips or feet, as in image 2.
> - **Match the mascot's style:** retro cartoon, flat colours, no gradients, the same thick dark outline (#2C2C2A) as the duck, simple rounded shapes, a slight print texture like the original.
> - **Follow the pose:** the back arm is bent behind him, the front arm is bent forward in a fist, the legs are mid-stride. Sleeves and gloves follow the arms and hands exactly.
> - **Colours (brand palette only):** Crew Navy #1B3F72, Track Blue #378ADD, Duck Gold #EF9F27, Pavement #F5F0E8, Asphalt #2C2C2A. The reflective vest may use safety yellow #F7D23E with white stripes.
> - The duck already wears a **blue headband and a blue singlet**. Tops and jackets must cover the whole singlet. Hats must cover the top of the head and the headband.
>
> Before we start, reply with just "Ready". The first item is next.

## If ChatGPT gets it wrong

- **It drew the whole duck:** reply *"Remove the duck completely. Keep only the [item] on a transparent background, in the same place and size."*
- **The background isn't transparent:** reply *"Make the background fully transparent (PNG with alpha), no white."*
- **The item is too big, too small or off-centre:** don't worry about small offsets. When the pieces come back, I fit each one onto the duck (scale and position) before adding it.
- **The style drifts after a few items:** start a new message with *"Same style as the toque you drew earlier"* and re-attach image 1.
